Units, Standards, and the SI System

Week 1 · Topic 1 · Reading · about 12 min

Every measurement in physics is a number paired with a unit. To compare results across labs and countries, scientists agreed on one set of standards: the International System of Units, or SI .

The SI defines seven base units. Mechanics needs only three of them: the meter for length, the kilogram for mass, and the second for time .

Textbook p. 5
“…the SI base units of length, mass, and time are the meter, kilogram, and second…”
Open page 5 in the book

Converting between units

Units can be treated like algebraic quantities that cancel. Multiply by a conversion factor equal to one, such as 1 in. = 2.54 cm, to change units without changing the quantity .
